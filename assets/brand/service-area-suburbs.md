## How this feeds SEO in posts
- Reference the suburb by name in the caption opening line when a post is
  location-specific (e.g. "Locked out in Milton? We're there fast.")
- Add a suburb hashtag alongside general hashtags (e.g. #MiltonLocksmith)
- Where possible, link back to the matching /service-areas/{suburb}/ page on
  fastfixlocksmith.com.au from the caption or GBP post's action button — this is
  what actually feeds SEO/topical relevance back to the site
- Don't make every post suburb-specific; mix general-topic posts (service type)
  with occasional suburb-specific ones per the brand brief
